BreakPay — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026. Developer: Osvald Studio. App: BreakPay (package com.osvaldstudio.breakpay).

Summary

BreakPay does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The app does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

Your work schedule, salary and currency settings, break timers and break history are stored only on your device so the app can estimate the value of your breaks. Android backup is disabled for this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Estimates only

BreakPay shows an estimate based on the numbers you enter. It does not know whether a break is paid under your contract and it does not pay or transfer money.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
