BreakPay — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026. Developer: Osvald Studio. App: BreakPay (iOS and Android).

Summary

BreakPay does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the app itself makes no network requests. Optional tips are paid through Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) and unlock nothing.

Data stored on your device

Your work schedule, salary and currency settings, break timers and break history are stored only on your device so the app can estimate the value of your breaks. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. On Android, app backup is disabled. On iOS, the app's data can be included in the device backups you choose to make (iCloud or a computer); you control this in iOS Settings.

Optional tips

The app offers optional one-time tips (and, on Android only, an optional monthly tip). Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. BreakPay receives no name, email or payment details, and tips unlock no features.

Estimates only

BreakPay shows an estimate based on the numbers you enter. It does not know whether a break is paid under your contract and it does not pay or transfer money.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
