Zen Sit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026. Developer: Osvald Studio. App: Zen Sit (package studio.osvald.zensit).

Summary

Zen Sit does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your progress only locally on your device: chosen character, interface language, points, unlocked places, wardrobe items and total sitting time. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Network access

When you unlock some places, the app downloads their video and sound files from a public file host (GitHub, operated by GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, the host may receive technical information such as your IP address to deliver the files; we do not receive or store it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Permissions

The app uses only the Internet permission (to download the files above) and keeps the screen awake while you sit in a scene. It does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
